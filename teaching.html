Teaching and supervision

I have supported fourteen modules in power and energy systems as a graduate assistant at the National University of Singapore (2020-2024) and a research and teaching assistant at Istanbul Technical University (2017-2020), including graduate modules at both. My teaching combines mathematical foundations, system-level understanding and simulation tools.
