About me

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the Power Systems Research Group at Newcastle University, working with Dr Matt Deakin, Dr David Greenwood and Prof Haris Patsios. I am also a visiting researcher at Özyeğin University. I received my PhD from the National University of Singapore in 2024, where I was advised by Prof Dipti Srinivasan. Prior to joining Newcastle, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at NUS.

My research centres on the operation, planning and coordination of low-carbon electricity networks, using mathematical optimisation and machine learning. My work spans EV charging, from single stations to large fleets, distribution system flexibility and TSO-DSO coordination, and the integration of low-carbon technologies into low-voltage networks.
